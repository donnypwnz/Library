Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Library is a Windows desktop app for organising product research. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short, your data stays on your PC and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives any of it.

Data stored on your PC

  • Your library (products, photos, suppliers, tags and notes) is stored on your own PC, in the app’s own folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%.
  • The app has no accounts and no servers. It contains no analytics, ads or tracking, and it doesn’t send your data to the developer or to anyone else.
  • You can delete everything at any time by deleting that folder.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app uses Google’s sign-in for desktop apps. Your Google password never goes through the app.

  • Permission: the app asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can see, create and change only the files it created itself: a folder of its own holding backup files of your library. It can’t see or read any other file in your Drive.
  • What it does with that access: it uploads backups of your library, lists them, restores one when you ask, and deletes the oldest backups so that only the number you choose is kept. It does nothing else with your Google account.
  • Your email address is read once, only so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Where the data goes: directly between your PC and Google. It doesn’t pass through any other server, and the developer never receives it.
  • Your sign-in is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC, not in a file.

Library’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to back up and restore your own library. It isn’t shared, sold, or used for ads, and no person reads it.

To stop: click Disconnect in the app, which removes the sign-in from your PC and from Google. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Other connections

  • Product photos are downloaded from the marketplaces’ image servers so they can be shown and compared. The app sends no personal data with these requests.
  • Your store’s product list is read from the store’s public product feed, only when you click to read it. No login is needed.

Children

The app isn’t meant for children and collects no information about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
