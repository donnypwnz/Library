Library

A small Windows app for organising product research: products, suppliers, photos and notes in one tidy library, with a check for products you already have.

What it does

  • Keeps a library of products you’re researching, with their photos, specs, suppliers and your notes.
  • Warns you when a new product looks like one you already have.
  • Backs up the library to a folder on your PC and, if you choose, to your own Google Drive.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything is stored on your own PC. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking.
  • Google Drive backup is optional. The app can only see the files it created itself in your Drive, nothing else.
